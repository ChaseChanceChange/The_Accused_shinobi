# The Accused — Shadow-Shinobi — Player-Facing Canon

This file contains the language and fantasy the player should encounter in the game. It exists so UI copy, onboarding, quests, menus, and future AI-authored content do not drift back toward the old setting.

## Core player promise

**The Accused — Shadow-Shinobi** is a persistent multiplayer action RPG about becoming an exceptional shinobi through combat, exploration, crafting, build decisions, mastery, and dangerous progression.

The player is not travelling through four elemental realms to awaken ancient crystals or Eidolons.

The player is a shinobi building a name, a style, a legend, and eventually something far beyond an ordinary warrior.

## Canonical onboarding direction

Preferred new-player message:

> **Test your skill. Shape your shadow. Forge your legend.**
>
> Begin your path as a shinobi, learn the arts of combat and crafting, and decide what kind of warrior you will become. Explore dangerous lands, take on missions, master techniques, gather rare materials, and forge gear that can become uniquely yours.
>
> The strongest shinobi are not simply handed power. They build it, risk it, lose it, and earn it again.

The exact wording can evolve for UI length, but the meaning should remain.

## Crafting identity

Crafting is a major part of the fantasy, not a side menu.

Players should be able to pursue increasingly powerful equipment while accepting meaningful resource costs and real risk. Rare results should feel personal and memorable.

Preferred language:

- forge
- refine
- temper
- infuse
- masterwork
- perfect
- rare materials
- dangerous recipes
- unique outcomes
- failed attempt
- recovered knowledge

Avoid making the system sound like generic fantasy blacksmithing when a stronger shinobi-specific term fits.

## Power ladder

The game may use multiple progression systems, but the end-state fantasy is:

**Shinobi → Master → Exalted → Ascendant → Transcendent**

These names are a working player-facing ladder, not yet the final mathematical progression model.

### Transcendent

**Transcendent** is the preferred official term for the level beyond ordinary god-tier power.

A Transcendent shinobi has crossed the boundary between exceptional mortal skill and something fundamentally greater. Reaching this state should be extremely difficult and uncommon.

Do not use "Saiyan" as a rank, and do not build the hierarchy around another franchise's terminology.

## Tone

The world should feel:

- dark
- dangerous
- stylish
- mysterious
- skill-driven
- competitive without being pay-to-win
- rewarding for players who experiment and master systems

The game can have humour and personality, but the core fantasy should still feel serious enough that reaching Transcendent status means something.

## Hard rule for legacy content

Remove or rewrite player-facing references to:

- Eidolons
- ancient crystals as the central objective
- Earth / Water / Fire / Air as the game's main identity
- saving an old named world or deity from the inherited setting

Those ideas may exist in old source material as implementation history, but they are not the player-facing fantasy of Shadow-Shinobi.

## What replaces the old introduction

Instead of:

> Journey through Earth, Water, Fire, and Air. Restore their ancient crystals, awaken the Eidolons, and confront the darkness beyond.

Use the Shadow-Shinobi concept:

> **Enter the world of The Accused — Shadow-Shinobi. Test your combat skill, master your techniques, gather rare materials, and forge the ultimate build. Rise from shinobi to Transcendent and decide what your legend becomes.**

The exact copy can be shortened for buttons, login screens, mobile layouts, or loading screens while preserving the same identity.

## Future content rule

Every new quest, faction, item, enemy, ability, zone, boss, cinematic, tutorial, and menu should answer one question:

**Does this feel like it belongs in The Accused — Shadow-Shinobi?**

If not, rewrite it before adding it to the player-facing game.
